We work for you.

Castle Road Construction was established in 1992. Our goal and focus has always been to provide the best remodeling experience to homeowners and local businesses in Harford County, MD.

We're a licensed and insured home improvement contractor, and while Harford County is home, we service all of Maryland. Roofing is where we built our name, and over three decades the work has grown to include windows, doors, siding, trim, gutters, decks, kitchens, baths, basements and storm damage repair.

What hasn't changed is how we work: we show up when we say we will, use materials we'd put on our own homes, and stand behind every job.
