Testimonials

Common questions

Don't see yours? Call [[phone]]

Are estimates really free? [[icon plus]]

Yes. We'll come out, look at the project with you and give you a written estimate at no cost and with no obligation.

Are you licensed and insured? [[icon plus]]

Yes. Castle Road Construction is licensed by the Maryland Home Improvement Commission (MHIC #121766) and fully insured. Our work is guaranteed.

What areas do you serve? [[icon plus]]

We're based in Bel Air and focus on Harford County, including Forest Hill, Fallston, Jarrettsville, Abingdon, Churchville, Aberdeen and Havre de Grace. We also take projects throughout Maryland.

Will you work with my insurance company? [[icon plus]]

Yes. For wind and storm damage we inspect and document the damage, meet with your adjuster and handle the repairs, so the claim is easier on you.

What roofing brands do you install? [[icon plus]]

We install GAF and Tamko roofing systems and accessories. We're a GAF Certified installer, a CertainTeed ShingleMaster and a Tamko Preferred Contractor.

Do you take on commercial projects? [[icon plus]]

Yes. Along with homeowners, we work with local businesses and property owners on roofing, siding and exterior projects.
